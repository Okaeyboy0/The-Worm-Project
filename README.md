# 🪱 The Worm Project

> *Can intelligence emerge from a simple digital organism through survival, learning, memory, adaptation, and evolution?*

The Worm Project is a long-term artificial life experiment that explores how increasingly complex behavior can emerge from simple rules.

Instead of starting with advanced AI or large language models, this project begins with a single digital organism that can perform only a handful of basic actions.

Over time, the organism will gain the ability to perceive its environment, remember experiences, learn from rewards and mistakes, evolve over generations, communicate, and eventually solve increasingly complex problems.

The goal is **not** to hard-code intelligence.

The goal is to create the conditions where intelligence may gradually emerge.

---

# Project Philosophy

Nature did not create intelligence overnight.

Simple organisms survived.

Some adapted.

Some evolved.

Over millions of years those small improvements produced increasingly intelligent life.

The Worm Project asks:

> **Can a similar process occur inside a digital world?**
